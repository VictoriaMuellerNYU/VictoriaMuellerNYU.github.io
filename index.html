About

I am a Biomedical Engineering student at NYU, interested in robotics and computational psychiatry. In BE-GY 6353, Intelligent Healthcare Systems and Technologies, I study how robots assist in cardiac and neurovascular catheter procedures. My coursework covers the parts these robots are built from: IMU sensors, DC motors, encoders and PID control. In lab, I connect real sensors and check what they report against the physics behind them.
