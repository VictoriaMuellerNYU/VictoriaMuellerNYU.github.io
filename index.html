Hi, I'm Victoria.

PhD candidate in Biomedical Engineering, NYU Tandon School of Engineering

I work in computational psychiatry. I build machine-learning and LLM-based systems that read affective state from face, speech, gaze, movement and physiology, with the aim of recognizing mental-health risk earlier.

My PhD is advised by Dr. Katharina Schultebraucks in the Department of Psychiatry at NYU Grossman School of Medicine. Before NYU, I studied Medical Engineering at FAU Erlangen-Nuremberg, spent an exchange semester at the Technion in Haifa, and did imaging research at Siemens Healthineers.

Away from the desk I'm a runner and play competitive tennis (former team captain and coach). I'm also interested in philosophy, meditation, and how wearables can help improve everyday health.
